# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, useful place for architecture and
software engineering work.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate clearly and respectfully;
- focus criticism on ideas, evidence and behavior;
- welcome different backgrounds and reasonable technical disagreement;
- protect private, confidential and security-sensitive information; and
- follow the project license and applicable laws.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate
disruption and publishing private information without permission are not
acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
repository's available private contact channel. Do not use a public issue for
sensitive reports. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove
content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
